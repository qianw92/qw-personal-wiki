# Menlo Park

Menlo Park is a city on the Peninsula, located south of Redwood City and north of Palo Alto, with East Palo Alto as its neighbor. It has a population of 33,780 as of the 2020 Census and is home to the headquarters of Meta (Facebook), as well as being adjacent to Stanford University's land holdings.
