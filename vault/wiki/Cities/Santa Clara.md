# Santa Clara

Santa Clara is a city located directly north of San Jose, situated between Sunnyvale and San Jose. It's home to Levi's Stadium, the San Francisco 49ers' stadium, and Santa Clara University.

## Facts
- Population: 127,647 (2020 Census).
- Location: directly north of San Jose, between Sunnyvale and San Jose.
- Highlight: home to Levi's Stadium (the San Francisco 49ers' home field) and Santa Clara University.
