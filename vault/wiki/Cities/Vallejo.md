# Vallejo

Vallejo is a city located at the northern tip of San Francisco Bay, across from Richmond and near the Carquinez Strait. It's a city of 126,090 people as of the 2020 census, and historically known as a naval shipyard town that was decommissioned in 1996. Now, Vallejo is a more affordable commuter city in the North Bay.

## Facts
- Population: 126,090 (2020 Census).
- Location: at the northern tip of San Francisco Bay, where it meets the Carquinez Strait, northeast of Richmond across the water.
- Highlight: a historic naval shipyard town (Mare Island Naval Shipyard, decommissioned 1996), now a more affordable North Bay commuter city.
