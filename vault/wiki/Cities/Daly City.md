# Daly City

Daly City is a city located immediately south of San Francisco on the Peninsula. It's known for its foggier hillside neighborhoods and serves as a major gateway suburb connecting San Francisco to the rest of the Peninsula. The 2020 Census recorded a population of 104,901 people in Daly City.

## Facts
- Population: 104,901 (2020 Census).
- Location: immediately south of San Francisco, the first city encountered driving south along the Peninsula on Highway 1 or I-280.
- Highlight: known for its foggy hillside neighborhoods and as a major gateway suburb connecting SF to the rest of the Peninsula.
