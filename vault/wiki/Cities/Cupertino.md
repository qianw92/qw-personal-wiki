# Cupertino

Cupertino is a city located southwest of Sunnyvale in the western South Bay, near the Santa Cruz Mountains. It's notable for being the headquarters of Apple, which includes the distinctive ring-shaped Apple Park campus. The 2020 census recorded a population of 60,381 people in Cupertino.

## Facts
- Population: 60,381 (2020 Census).
- Location: southwest of Sunnyvale, in the western South Bay near the base of the Santa Cruz Mountains.
- Highlight: headquarters of Apple, including the ring-shaped Apple Park campus.
