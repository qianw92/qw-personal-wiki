# Hayward

Hayward is a city located on the east shore of the Bay, between Oakland/San Leandro and Fremont. It has a population of 162,954 as of the 2020 census and is now a residential hub with California State University, East Bay, alongside its historical industrial and agricultural roots. The city is connected to the Peninsula by the San Mateo Bridge.

## Facts
