# San Mateo

San Mateo is a Peninsula suburb located south of San Francisco and south of South San Francisco, roughly halfway between the two cities. It has a population of 105,661 as of the 2020 Census and is known for its walkable downtown and the San Mateo Bridge, which connects it to the East Bay.

## Facts
- Population: 105,661 (2020 Census).
- Location: on the Peninsula, south of San Francisco and south of South San Francisco, roughly halfway between SF and Palo Alto.
- Highlight: a central Peninsula suburb with a walkable downtown and the San Mateo Bridge connecting east to the East Bay.
