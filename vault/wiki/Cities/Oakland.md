# Oakland

Oakland is the largest city in the East Bay, located directly across the San Francisco Bay from San Francisco and immediately south of Berkeley. It's a vibrant city with a working port, a diverse population, and a distinct downtown and arts scene that sets it apart from San Francisco.

## Facts
- Population: 440,646 (2020 Census).
- Location: directly across San Francisco Bay from SF (east of SF), reachable via the Bay Bridge; immediately south of Berkeley.
- Highlight: the East Bay's largest city, with a working port, a diverse population, and its own distinct downtown and arts scene separate from SF.
