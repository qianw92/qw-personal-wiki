# Concord

Concord is a large city located inland in the East Bay, north of Walnut Creek and further from San Francisco than many other Bay Area cities. It's the largest city in Contra Costa County and is known as a commuter suburb with a central park and amphitheater.

## Facts
- Population: 125,410 (2020 Census).
- Location: inland East Bay, north of Walnut Creek, further from SF than most Bay-shore cities.
- Highlight: the largest city in Contra Costa County, a commuter suburb with a large central park and amphitheater.
