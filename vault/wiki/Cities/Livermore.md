# Livermore

Livermore is the easternmost major city in the Bay Area, located east of Pleasanton and on the edge of the Bay Area before the Central Valley. It's a significant location, being home to Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory and a developing wine region called the Livermore Valley.

## Facts
- Population: 87,955 (2020 Census).
- Location: the easternmost major Bay Area city, east of Pleasanton, at the edge of the Bay Area before the Central Valley.
- Highlight: home to Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory and a growing wine-country region (the Livermore Valley).
