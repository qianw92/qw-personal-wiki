# Walnut Creek

Walnut Creek is a suburban retail and dining hub located inland in the East Bay, northeast of Oakland and Berkeley. It sits over the hills from the Bay shoreline and has a population of 70,127 as of the 2020 Census. Because of its location, Walnut Creek is less frequently fog-covered than cities facing the Bay.

## Facts

- Location: inland East Bay, northeast of Oakland and Berkeley, over the hills from the Bay shoreline.
- Highlight: a suburban retail and dining hub for the inland East Bay ("Contra Costa County"), less foggy than the Bay-facing cities.
