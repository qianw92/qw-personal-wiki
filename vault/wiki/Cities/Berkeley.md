# Berkeley

Berkeley is a city located on the east shore of the Bay, north of Oakland and directly across the Bay from San Francisco and the Golden Gate. It's home to the University of California, Berkeley, and has a long history as a center of activism and academic research, with a population of 124,321 as of the 2020 Census.
