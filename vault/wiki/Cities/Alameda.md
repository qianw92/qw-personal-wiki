# Alameda

Alameda is an island city located just south of Oakland, connected by bridges and a tunnel across the estuary. It's a former naval air station site that now has preserved Victorian-era housing and a small-town feel, making it close to Oakland. The population of Alameda was 78,280 in the 2020 Census.

## Facts
- Population: 78,280 (2020 Census).
- Location: an island city just south of Oakland, connected by bridges and a tunnel, across the estuary from Oakland's downtown.
