# Sunnyvale

Sunnyvale is a city located south of Mountain View, between Mountain View and Santa Clara, with a population of 155,805 as of the 2020 Census. It's a major Silicon Valley tech employment center, and historically known for defense and semiconductor firms, but now home to many software companies.
