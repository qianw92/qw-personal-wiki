# San Rafael

San Rafael is a city in Marin County, located north of San Francisco across the Golden Gate Bridge. It serves as the government and commercial center for Marin County, situated at the base of hills that lead up to Mount Tamalpais. The city had a population of 61,271 in 2020.

## Facts
- Population: 61,271 (2020 Census).
- Location: in Marin County, north of San Francisco across the Golden Gate Bridge, the largest city in Marin.
- Highlight: Marin County's government and commercial center, at the base of hills leading to Mount Tamalpais.
