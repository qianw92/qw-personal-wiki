# Redwood City

Redwood City is a city located on the Peninsula, situated south of San Mateo and north of Palo Alto. It has a population of 84,292 as of the 2020 Census and is notable for being home to a major Oracle campus, although Oracle's headquarters moved to Austin, Texas in 2020.

## Facts
- Population: 84,292 (2020 Census).
- Location: on the Peninsula, south of San Mateo and north of Palo Alto.
- Highlight: home to a major Oracle campus (Oracle's corporate headquarters relocated to Austin, Texas in December 2020).
