# Richmond

Richmond is an industrial port city located on the east shore of the Bay, north of Berkeley. It's connected to Marin County by the Richmond-San Rafael Bridge and is known for its major oil refinery and ongoing waterfront redevelopment.

## Facts
- Population: 116,448 (2020 Census).
- Location: on the east shore of the Bay, north of Berkeley, connected to Marin County by the Richmond-San Rafael Bridge.
- Highlight: an industrial port city with a major oil refinery and growing waterfront redevelopment.
