# Mountain View

Mountain View is a city located south of Palo Alto, on the southwest edge of the Bay, between the Peninsula and the South Bay. It's a major hub for technology, serving as the headquarters for Google (Alphabet) and featuring a downtown Castro Street popular with tech workers.

## Facts
- Population: 82,376 (2020 Census).
- Location: south of Palo Alto, on the southwest edge of the Bay, between the Peninsula and the South Bay.
- Highlight: headquarters of Google (Alphabet), with a downtown Castro Street known for tech-worker foot traffic.
