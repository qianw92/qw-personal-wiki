# Milpitas

Milpitas is a city located northeast of San Jose, along the eastern shore of the South Bay. It has a population of 80,273 as of the 2020 Census and is known for being a smaller tech-manufacturing hub with the Great Mall, one of the Bay Area's largest shopping malls.

## Facts
- Population: 80,273 (2020 Census).
- Location: northeast of San Jose, at the northern tip of the South Bay along the eastern shore.
- Highlight: a smaller tech-manufacturing hub with one of the Bay Area's largest shopping malls, the Great Mall.
