# Fremont

Fremont is a city located at the southeastern edge of the Bay, south of Oakland and Hayward. It's directly across the Bay from Palo Alto and Menlo Park and is home to Tesla's main vehicle assembly plant, as well as a large and diverse South Asian immigrant community.

## Facts
- Population: 230,504 (2020 Census).
- Location: at the southeastern edge of the Bay, south of Oakland/Hayward, directly across the Bay from Palo Alto and Menlo Park.
- Highlight: home to Tesla's primary vehicle assembly plant and a large, diverse South Asian immigrant community.
