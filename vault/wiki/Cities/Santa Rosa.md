# Santa Rosa

Santa Rosa is the largest city in the North Bay, located in Sonoma County to the northwest of Napa. It serves as the commercial and government center for the region, and is situated at the heart of Sonoma County's wine and agricultural areas.

## Facts
- Population: 178,127 (2020 Census).
- Location: in Sonoma County, northwest of Napa and the largest city in the North Bay.
- Highlight: the North Bay's commercial and government center, at the heart of Sonoma County's wine and agricultural region.
