# Sunset District

The Sunset District is a large residential area in southwest San Francisco, located south of Golden Gate Park and extending down to the Pacific Ocean. It's particularly known for its foggy weather, rows of houses that look alike, and the presence of Ocean Beach.

## Facts
- Location: southwest SF, south of Golden Gate Park, stretching from the park to the Pacific Ocean.
- Highlight: the city's largest residential district, known for foggy weather, rows of similar houses, and Ocean Beach.
