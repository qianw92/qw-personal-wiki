# The Mission

The Mission District is a historically Latino neighborhood in south-central San Francisco, located south of the Tenderloin and Hayes Valley and east of the Castro. It's a vibrant area famous for its murals, taquerias, and sunny climate.

## Facts
- Location: south-central SF, south of the Tenderloin/Hayes Valley area and east of the Castro.
- Highlight: a historically Latino neighborhood known for murals, taquerias, and one of the city's sunniest microclimates.
