# Potrero Hill

Potrero Hill is a neighborhood in southeast San Francisco, located south of Mission Bay and east of the Mission District. It's known for being a sunnier hillside neighborhood with sweeping views of the Bay, and its base has a history rooted in industry.

## Facts
