# The Presidio

The Presidio is a former U.S. Army post located in the northwest corner of San Francisco, right at the foot of the Golden Gate Bridge. Today, it's a national park offering forests, trails, and coastal bluffs for visitors to explore.

## Facts
- Location: northwest corner of SF, at the foot of the Golden Gate Bridge.
- Highlight: a former U.S. Army post, now a national park with forests, trails, and coastal bluffs.
