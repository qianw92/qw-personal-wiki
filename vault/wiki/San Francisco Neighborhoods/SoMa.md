# SoMa

SoMa is a neighborhood in central-east San Francisco, right south of Market Street and the Financial District. It's a place that used to be industrial, but now features a mix of tech company offices, the Moscone Center for conventions, and museums.

## Facts
- Location: central-east SF, immediately south of Market Street and the Financial District.
- Highlight: a former industrial area now home to tech company offices, convention space (Moscone Center), and museums.
