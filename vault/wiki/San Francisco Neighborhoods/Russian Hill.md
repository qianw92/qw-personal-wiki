# Russian Hill

Russian Hill is a neighborhood in north San Francisco, located west of North Beach and north of Nob Hill. It's famous for its steep, winding streets, most notably the Lombard Street switchback.

## Facts
- Location: north SF, west of North Beach and north of Nob Hill, close to the northern waterfront.
- Highlight: known for its steep, winding streets, including the famous switchback block of Lombard Street.
