# Hayes Valley

Hayes Valley is a neighborhood in central San Francisco, located west of Civic Center and north of the Mission District. It's known for its boutique shopping and dining options, which developed in the 1990s after a freeway was removed.

## Facts
- Location: central SF, west of Civic Center and north of the Mission District.
- Highlight: a boutique shopping and dining strip that emerged after a freeway removal opened up the area in the 1990s.
