# Excelsior

Excelsior is a diverse neighborhood in south-central San Francisco, located south of the Mission District and east of John McLaren Park. It's a largely residential area, centered along Mission Street, and is known for being a working-class neighborhood.

## Facts
- Location: south-central SF, south of the Mission District, bordered by John McLaren Park to the east.
- Highlight: a diverse, largely residential working-class neighborhood in the city's southern flatlands, centered along Mission Street.
