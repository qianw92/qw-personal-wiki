# Telegraph Hill

Telegraph Hill is located in northeast San Francisco, east of North Beach, and it offers panoramic views of the northern waterfront and the Bay. The hill is particularly notable for being topped by Coit Tower, which provides excellent views over the Financial District and the Bay Bridge.

## Facts
- Location: northeast SF, east of North Beach, overlooking the northern waterfront and Bay.
- Highlight: topped by Coit Tower, with views over the Financial District and the Bay Bridge.
