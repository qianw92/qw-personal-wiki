# Dogpatch

Dogpatch is a neighborhood in southeast San Francisco located along the waterfront, south of Mission Bay and east of Potrero Hill. It's a former shipbuilding and industrial area that has been repurposed into converted warehouse spaces and galleries.

## Facts
- Location: southeast SF, along the waterfront south of Mission Bay and east of Potrero Hill.
