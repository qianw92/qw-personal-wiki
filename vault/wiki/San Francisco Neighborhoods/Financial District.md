# Financial District

The Financial District is a compact area in northeast San Francisco, located just south of the Embarcadero waterfront. It's known as the city's traditional banking and corporate headquarters district, and it features the densest cluster of skyscrapers in San Francisco.

## Facts
- Location: northeast SF, the compact high-rise core just south of the Embarcadero waterfront.
- Highlight: the region's traditional banking and corporate headquarters district, and the densest cluster of skyscrapers in the city.
