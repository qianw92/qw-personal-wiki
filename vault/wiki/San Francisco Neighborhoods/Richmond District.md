# Richmond District

Richmond District is a residential area in northwest San Francisco, located north of Golden Gate Park and extending to the Pacific Ocean. It's known for being a foggy neighborhood with a significant Asian-American population, and Clement Street has a popular shopping area.

## Facts
- Location: northwest SF, north of Golden Gate Park, stretching from the park to the Pacific Ocean.
- Highlight: a foggy, residential district with a large Asian-American population and Clement Street's shopping corridor.
