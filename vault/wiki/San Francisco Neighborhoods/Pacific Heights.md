# Pacific Heights

The Pacific Heights neighborhood is located in north San Francisco, west of Russian Hill and north of Japantown. It's a wealthy neighborhood famous for its grand mansions and stunning views overlooking the Bay.

## Facts
- Location: north SF, west of Russian Hill and north of Japantown, on a ridge overlooking the Bay.
- Highlight: one of the city's wealthiest neighborhoods, known for grand mansions and Bay views.
