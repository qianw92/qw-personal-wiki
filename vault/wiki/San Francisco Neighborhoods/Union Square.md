# Union Square

Union Square is a popular area in central-north San Francisco, located just south of the Financial District. It's known as the city's main shopping and theater district, with a large public plaza at its center.

## Facts
- Location: central-north SF, just south of the Financial District's western edge.
- Highlight: the city's main shopping and theater district, centered on a public plaza.
