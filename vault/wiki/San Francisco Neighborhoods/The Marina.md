# The Marina

The Marina is a flat neighborhood in north San Francisco, located along the waterfront west of Russian Hill and the Presidio. It's a popular spot for jogging and boasts views of the Golden Gate Bridge, as well as yacht harbors.

## Facts
- Location: north SF, along the northern waterfront, west of Russian Hill and the Presidio's eastern edge.
- Highlight: a flat, waterfront neighborhood with views of the Golden Gate Bridge, popular for jogging and yacht harbors.
