# Golden Gate Park

Golden Gate Park is a large, landscaped park located in west-central San Francisco, stretching from the Richmond to the Sunset districts. It's a significant green space in the city, bigger than New York's Central Park, and features museums, gardens, and open lawns. The park also acts as a boundary between the northwest and southwest neighborhoods of San Francisco.

## Facts
- Location: west-central SF, spanning the width of the city between the Richmond and Sunset districts.
- Highlight: a large landscaped park (bigger than New York's Central Park) with museums, gardens, and open lawns, forming the boundary between the northwest and southwest neighborhoods.
